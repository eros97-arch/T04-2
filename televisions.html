Television Energy Consumption

Explore television energy consumption in the Australian market and discover how screen technology, screen size and brands can differ in their energy use.

Exploring the Australian TV Market

The dataset contains information about television models available in Australia, including screen technology, screen size, average power consumption and star rating.

The visualisations below explore these characteristics to identify patterns in the television market and help consumers understand differences between TV models.

1. What TV technologies are available?

The dataset contains three main screen technologies: LCD, LCD (LED) and OLED. LCD (LED) models are the most frequently represented, followed by LCD and OLED.

2. What screen sizes are most common?

The dataset contains a wide range of screen sizes. The most frequently represented sizes include approximately 65, 55 and 75 inches, showing that larger televisions are strongly represented in the available models.

3. Which brands have the greatest number of different models?

The dataset contains models from many different brands. KOGAN has the greatest number of models, followed by LG and SAMSUNG ELECTRONICS.

4. Which screen technology consumes the least power?

LCD has the lowest average power consumption at approximately 85.7 W, compared with LCD (LED) at 120.5 W and OLED at 128.8 W.

5. Does screen size affect power consumption?

The scatter plot shows that power consumption generally increases as screen size becomes larger. However, there is considerable variation between individual TV models, meaning screen size is not the only factor affecting power use.

6. Is there a relationship between screen size and star rating?

The data shows that star ratings vary across different screen sizes. The points are widely distributed, suggesting that screen size alone does not strongly determine a television's energy rating.

7. How does power consumption differ between brands?

Average power consumption varies between brands. Some brands have considerably higher average power consumption than others, showing that brand and model selection can make a difference when comparing television energy use.

Key Takeaways

Screen Technology

LCD (LED) is the most frequently represented screen technology in the dataset.

Screen Size

Larger screen sizes, particularly around 55 to 75 inches, are strongly represented.

Brands

KOGAN has the greatest number of models, followed by LG and SAMSUNG ELECTRONICS.

Power Consumption

LCD has the lowest average power consumption among the three screen technologies.

Screen Size vs Power

Larger screens generally use more power, although individual models vary considerably.

Energy Rating

Screen size does not appear to have a strong relationship with star rating by itself.
